NewsTide · how it is built

Two small pieces on Dima's Mac: a newspaper page in the browser and a little server that reads the news and asks the AI. Start with npm start, open localhost:3000.

Architecture · click a step to trace it

Green boxes run on the Mac. White boxes are outside services. The orange border costs money.

Components · where each PRD behavior lives

Page and Indicator View public/

Masthead, market strip, coin field, Long/Short, timeframe, Get data with a 60 s countdown, result. Arrow up/down/sideways with bull/bear/hamster and green/red/yellowish paper; light green/yellow/red from plan_fit. PRD: Screens and Layout, The indicator (result), Waiting for the result.

Cost Counter public/app.js

"This analysis: $0.02 · This session: $0.10". Computed from the token counts the model reports and config/prices.json. Resets on reload. Shows the business idea of a visible balance.

Market Strip Service server/market.js

BTC from CoinGecko, Nasdaq from Yahoo, 3 crypto headlines from the last 24 h, 3 macro items from the last 7 days. Cached 5 minutes. PRD: Market strip.

Coin Registry config/coins.json

ETH, SOL, AAVE with their official feeds. A new coin is one new entry. PRD: Coin input.

Feed Collector server/collect.js

Reads official + market feeds, keeps 24 h (below 4h) or 14 days (4h and above), removes duplicates, about 30 numbered items. Sample mode reads a saved, labelled snapshot so tests are free and repeatable. PRD: Background gathering.

Analyst server/analyst.js

Sends the numbered items, coin, plan and horizon to the model with a fixed answer shape: direction, plan_fit, verdict, spot/futures note, hidden chain citing item numbers.

Answer Checker server/check.js · test/check.test.js

Fields valid, verdict ≤ 6 words and no percent, light agrees with arrow and plan, every chain step cites a real item. This is how we check the model did what we meant.

Run Log runs/

Each run saved as a file with the full hidden chain and cost, so Dima can read why the indicator said what it said.
