NewsTide

Before a trade: pick a coin, long or short, a timeframe. Get one simple verdict on where the tide of news is carrying the coin.

What the scope became

Scope said "one short conclusion, simple as an RSI". Now we know exactly what that means: an arrow for direction, a traffic light for "with your plan or against it", a few words, a bull, a bear or a hamster, and the whole page changes color.

Behaviors and how we'll know they work

Coin input 3 coins, with suggestions
  • Typing "et", "so" or "aa" suggests ETH, SOL or AAVE
  • Only coins from the list can be submitted
Plan and timeframe below 4h intraday, 4h+ medium-term
  • Long/Short and a timeframe can be chosen after the coin
  • Below 4h gives an intraday analysis; 4h and above gives a medium-term analysis
Waiting time remaining, up to a minute
  • After Get data, the page shows the time remaining
  • The result appears within one minute
The indicator arrow · light · few words · animal · page tint
  • Result shows an arrow, a traffic light and a verdict of a few words
  • Long + bearish background: arrow down, red light, cheerful bear, red page
  • Long + bullish background: arrow up, green light, smiling bull, light green page
  • Neutral: arrow sideways, yellow light, hamster, light yellowish page
  • No percent anywhere
Hidden gathering the engine nobody sees
  • Coin's official open Telegram channel
  • Coin's X, through the user's connected X
  • Macro sites (for example Investing)
  • General crypto news
  • A logical chain inside: factor to factor, down to the coin, plus the coin's own plans and news
Market strip and X connection
  • Top of the page shows Bitcoin price, Nasdaq, top news of the day and week
  • First use offers to connect X and explains why
  • Without X: verdict from the other sources plus a note that X is one of the main sources

Look and feel

An old printed newspaper being opened: news columns, a newspaper feel, clearly about crypto and news, not a typical AI-generated site. The bull, bear, hamster and arrows are custom newspaper-style illustrations made with Higgsfield (credit cost checked in the spec first).
