NewsTide

Before a trade, a crypto trader states their plan, and the assistant pulls the whole news background into one short conclusion: does it support the plan or work against it, and why.

The unique kernel

A news background indicator as simple to read as RSI is for anyone (simplicity only, it need not look like RSI), with as few moving parts as possible. News, macro data, statements, geopolitics and the coin's own project news, all reduced to one short, concrete conclusion tailored to how you trade, and checked against the trade you are about to make.

User · core loop · proof

The timeframe decides the analysis

Below 4h · intradayNews and statements of the day, and those expected later today, that could move the price.
4h and above · medium-termProject data, community, macro, geopolitics and the wider background, plus what may happen next.

What "working" looks like

He picks ETH, SOL or AAVE, enters long or short and the timeframe, and within a minute sees a short conclusion, whether the background supports or contradicts his plan, and a short spot vs futures note.

The "oh, that's cool" beat: about twenty scattered headlines collapse into one line anyone gets at a glance. "Your plan is long, the background says down, because of X."

Boundary

Now in the proof of concept
  • Coins: ETH, SOL, AAVE
  • Both horizons, chosen by timeframe
  • One view for spot and futures, with a note where they may differ
  • A separate simple web page
  • Input: coin, long/short, timeframe. Output: short conclusion, verdict vs plan, spot/futures note
Later worth doing, not now
  • Any coin from the top 100
  • A TradingView indicator: reads the coin and timeframe on the chart, may show arrows and an RSI-like line. Needs an outside service, because Pine Script cannot fetch news or call an AI model (to verify in the spec)
Cut with reasons
  • Separate spot and futures analyses: one view with a note is enough
  • Long explanations: the learner wants a few words, not reading
  • A guaranteed correct forecast: no tool can promise it, and a video cannot prove it
Open for the PRD decide next
  • Probability in percent: a model would invent the number. Remove it, replace with words, or show both with a label
  • Visual form of the indicator: simple, clear for anyone, not necessarily RSI-like
  • Whether the ~20 collected items are visible at all

Why it matters to the learner

It targets his weakest spot as a trader: how news, geopolitics, macro data and the stock market move crypto. "It often goes completely differently from how you plan." And he wants to build something other people will use and enjoy.
